Confused Shrimp

Character development, storyboard, animation

Confused Shrimp" is a short, humorous animation featuring a shrimp displaying peculiar and comical behavior in its environment. The animation captures the essence of confusion through the shrimp's interactions, creating an amusing and light-hearted experience for viewers. This playful piece highlights my ability to bring characters to life with personality and humor.
