Trouble does not come alone

Character development, storyboard, animation

"Trouble does not comes alone" is a short animation depicting a series of unfortunate events happening one after another to the main character. It humorously illustrates how troubles often seem to come in clusters, using visual gags and slapstick humor. The main idea behind creating these comedic situations was inspired by the Ukrainian proverb "Біда не приходить одна," which means "Trouble does not come alone."
